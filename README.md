📖 About

The-List-of-Numbers-Tree is a custom incremental game where you progress through layers of numbers, sequences, and mathematical progression systems. Watch your numbers grow, unlock new prestige layers, and optimize your path to higher values!
• Custom About Text Example: In this game, numbers aren’t just counters—they are the entire universe. Buy generators, unlock new rows of milestones, and reach higher numbers than you ever thought possible.

🎮 Features

• Multiple Layers & Prestige Mechanics: Reset your progress strategically to gain powerful multipliers.
• Custom Number Systems: Built on top of the flexible The-Modding-Tree framework.
• Incremental Progression: Idle and active playstyles supported as you scale your numbers.
